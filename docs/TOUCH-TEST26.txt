Android touch test 26 - report 900024 combat corrections

Changes
- Descending paratroopers are exposed air targets for mobile AA and base
  defenses, rather than hidden transport passengers. Ground-only weapons
  cannot engage them until landing. Passengers inside transports stay hidden.
- Parachute height is included in combat range, defense projectile launch,
  missile tracking and damage calculations, including bridge deck offset.
- Turretless vehicle muzzle effects and projectile launch direction follow
  the hull. Aircraft use their body heading. Turreted vehicles retain turret
  heading and art TurretOffset; turretless effects no longer add that offset.
- Save format remains 52. Existing format-52 saves remain compatible.

Scope / remaining report items
This is a targeted combat and muzzle correction, not a complete Yuri release.
Gatling three-stage spin-up, unsupported superweapons, superweapon visual
timing, and Slave Miner relocation remain open. Do not describe these as fixed.
No renderer replacement or thermal tuning is included. Phone visual parity
requires device testing; CPU log samples are not GPU frame timing.

Phone checks
1. Drop enemy paratroopers near Flak Cannons and mobile AA. Confirm firing
   during descent, and ground-only guns engaging after touchdown.
2. Confirm infantry inside a transport cannot be attacked as passengers.
3. Fire a turretless vehicle in different headings and compare muzzle effect
   and projectile direction. Compare a turreted tank and aircraft as controls.
4. Share a game report naming the specific unit/action for remaining effects.

No retail assets are bundled. Only the Omar-based ra2-engine is changed.
