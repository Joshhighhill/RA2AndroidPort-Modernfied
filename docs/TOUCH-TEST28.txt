Android touch test 28 — sensor intentions and Yuri firing effects

Requires your own Red Alert 2 / Yuri's Revenge game files. No retail assets
are included. Based on Omar El Arab's 0BSD RA2 Engine.

Changes since test 27:
- Psychic Sensor intent lines can cross fog when the commanded destination
  is inside a powered allied sensor's radius. Units/map cells are not revealed.
- Ground force-fire uses the normal projectile flight and muzzle solver for
  weapons with flying projectiles; damage arrives on impact. Instant weapons
  remain instant. Ground missiles retain a stationary cell target.
- Gatling tanks charge while force-firing at ground or explicitly targeted
  friendly units/buildings; ground fire activates the correct stage weapon.
  Tank and defense HVA barrel frames continue while charge remains positive,
  instead of freezing whenever the short muzzle-flash timer ends.
- DiskLaser=yes now produces a weapon-coloured ring attack effect. This is
  a renderer approximation, not a verified pixel-perfect original recreation.
- Ground-target beam, tracer and impact effects resolve the selected cell.
- Bunker movement blends between simulation ticks, keeps exiting tanks between
  bunker layers and blends toward their current position after new orders.

Save format remains 53, compatible with test 27. Older format 52 saves remain
incompatible, as before. Existing saved files are not deleted.

Phone checks needed:
1. Put an enemy outside sight and order it toward a powered sensor's radius;
   check the intent line. Cut power and confirm the line disappears.
2. Force-fire a Lasher/Rhino at empty ground: shell leaves its muzzle, travels
   toward the selected cell and explodes on arrival, without an early hit.
3. Sustain Gatling Tank fire on ground and a durable target. Compare barrel
   motion, muzzle flash and spin-up stages with the reference video. Repeat
   automatic combat with the Gatling Cannon against ground/air targets.
4. Fire a Flying Disc at ground, infantry, vehicles and aircraft. Check the
   coloured ring starts at the airborne disc and connects to the target.
5. Enter/exit a Tank Bunker repeatedly, then order movement immediately after
   evacuation. Check layering, smooth movement and absence of position snaps.

No phone was attached for on-device visual verification. Automated tests and
Android build results accompany this release; they do not establish original
game visual parity. New report/video files remain private local references.
