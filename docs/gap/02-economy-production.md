# Gap analysis 02 — Economy, production, base building (REAL PLAY)

Read-only audit of what the player actually gets in a skirmish started from the
menu (`MainMenuScene` → `SkirmishSetupScene` → `GameScene`, `engine/main.cpp:6764`)
or `--interactive` (`run_interactive`, `main.cpp:5267`), versus the recovered
original behaviour in `docs/re/*.md`. Every "clone" claim below was traced through
the interactive code path (`launch_skirmish` `main.cpp:11557` + `GameScene`
`main.cpp:4343`), not a `--demo-*` gate. No source was modified.

## The root cause in one paragraph

`launch_skirmish` (`main.cpp:11560-11574`) builds the World with
`configure_skirmish_houses` + `set_building_types(ba.roster)` and **never calls
`set_economy`/`wire_economy`**. `World::types_` therefore stays empty, and
`World::step` skips **both** `ore_.step` and `step_harvest` behind
`if (!types_.empty())` (`engine/world.cpp:3219-3231`). The comments at
`world.hpp:646-657` and `main.cpp:11568-11573` state this was done on purpose to
keep `skirmish_hash`/`maps_hash` stable ("must NOT arm ore growth/harvest
stepping"). Every gate that exercises the economy (`--demo-econ`, `--demo-ai-*`,
`--demo-saveload`'s `sl_build_skirmish` at `main.cpp:15358-15359`) calls
`wire_economy` explicitly after building its world; the real game is the only
caller that does not. Result: harvesters spawn and sit still, ore never grows or
depletes, `take_income(0)` returns 0 forever, and the only ways money ever
*enters* the wallet in a real game are selling buildings and crates. The
production side (queues, placement, buildup, prerequisites, repair, sell) is
wired and works; it just has nothing to spend after the starting cash.

## Top 10 blockers (ordered by impact on being able to play)

1. **Economy firewall shut in the real game** (rows 7–9, 29). `launch_skirmish` never calls `wire_economy`, so no harvesting, no ore growth, no income for the player **or the AI**. The whole base-building loop is a one-shot spend of the starting cash. Fix is a single call in `GameScene` (the `--demo-saveload` harness already does exactly this at `main.cpp:15359`). S.
2. **No way to rebuild the economy or the base** (rows 1, 8). HARV/CMIN and AMCV/SMCV are not producible; when the free miner dies the economy is permanently gone, and when the ConYard dies the game is over. S for the units, M for deploy.
3. **Starting credits doubled and the lobby slider ignored for the human** (row 2). `mp_money()` + `starting_cash` both land in the wallet via `take_income` draining the seeded pool. S.
4. **Only 5 Allied units are producible in real play** (row 17); 36 recovered units (incl. every Soviet player unit) load only in gates. M.
5. **Soviet/Yuri human is broken** (row 19): Soviet base, Allied sidebar, Allied roster, Allied factory ids. Yuri selectable with no content. S.
6. **Superweapons granted at frame 0 without a silo; SW buildings inert** (row 25). Allied player nukes the AI at 10 minutes with no Chronosphere/Weather Control gameplay. M.
7. **Six placeable-but-dead buildings** (rows 16, 23, 24, 26, 28): GAYARD (land-placeable, no ships), GAAIRC (no aircraft), GADEPT, GACSPH, GAGAP, GAOREP. Naval/aircraft production is gate-only. L for naval/air, S to hide the rest.
8. **Lobby Tech Level and country do nothing** (rows 15, 18); the sidebar filter is a two-letter prefix test that also drops GTGCAN/ATESLA. S.
9. **Low-power secondary effects missing** (row 6): radar, powered defenses and SW charge keep working with no power. S.
10. **Walls build at normal speed with no line fill; no EVA production feedback** (rows 22, 27). S.
