# Wave-3 gap analysis 02: the Soviet campaign (SOV01-SOV12), visuals

Read-only audit of master `2c8616c` (2026-10-07). Worktree `D:\ra2-wt\w3-soviet`
(branch `w3-soviet`, Release build `build-rel`). Nothing is committed. Engine
source is unchanged except for one scratch cheat that is not for commit
(`engine/game_scene_play.inc`: `cheat w3reveal` reveals the map, and `cheat w3artdump` lists each
live building type with `BuildingArt.ok` and its frame count, plus each live unit image).
Original: RA2 1.006 data on `D:\Red Alert 2` (ra2.mix, expand01.mix
`art.ini`/`rules.ini`, ecache01.mix, language.mix).

Scope: what the 12 Soviet missions **look like**. The previous wave made all 24
missions winnable through their triggers (`docs/gap/wave2/01-campaign.md`). Nobody
had audited the art.

## Summary

The missions are mostly drawn. Across the 12 maps the census finds 1,615
structures, 417 vehicles, 485 infantry, 6,167 terrain objects, 8,140 overlay
cells and 107 smudges. All terrain, overlay and smudge art resolves, and the
mission ActiveAnims run (flags wave, oil derricks pump, the drive-in screen
plays). The user's report is still accurate, and it has a small number of root causes:

1. **Six landmarks are invisible, and four of them are the mission objectives.**
   These are the Eiffel Tower (SOV05 "Energize the PARIS TOWER"), the Arc de
   Triomphe (SOV05), the White House (SOV08 target), the Washington Monument, and
   the Lincoln and Jefferson Memorials (SOV08). Their art is `cg*.shp` in
   **`isogen.mix`**, and no clone resolver searches that mix (R1). The player sees an empty
   plaza. In SOV08 there is a black tile where the monument stands.
2. **Snow missions draw temperate civilian art.** In SOV04, SOV07, SOV11 and SOV12
   there are 171 civilian `CA*` buildings (cottages, farms, silos, Kremlin palace and towers, airport,
   outpost, oil derricks, flags), plus NACLON/NAPSIS/GAGATE_A. They show green roofs and
   grass pads on snow because the campaign type loader hard-codes the
   temperate chain (R1). For urban maps the temperate twin is pixel-identical in
   frames 0-2, so urban maps only lose the occupied+damaged frame.
3. **115 invisible light posts are drawn as gold "trophy" sprites** (`GALITE`),
   and the coloured light they cast is missing. These are `IN*LMP`/`NEGLAMP`/`INGALITE` with `InvisibleInGame=yes` in 9 missions. Night
   missions SOV01, SOV07 and SOV11 therefore have no street lighting (R2, R10).
4. **Most of the animation systems are missing.** Infantry have only
   Ready/Walk/FireUp/Die1: there is no Idle fidget, civilian Panic, Prone/Crawl, Cheer,
   Die2-5 or warhead `InfDeath` anims (R5). Buildings lack IdleAnim, SpecialAnim and
   ProductionAnim, the garrison frames, and rubble (R3, R4). The trigger-driven
   anims are not executed (`PlayAnimAt` ARRWS objective arrows, DEATH_E, WAKE1, particle smoke,
   lightning strikes, camera moves), and neither are radar events or sidebar flashes (R6).
   Map-placed Kirovs sit on the ground (R7).
5. Presentation outside the map is mostly fine. The loading screen, briefing text and
   objectives render correctly. Every `Intro=/Brief=` movie and the 26 movie trigger actions (25× action 100, 1× action 10) are
   unplayable because `MOVIES01/02.MIX` are 4-byte stubs on this install (a data limit, not a code gap).
   Mission speech could not be checked under `--play`, because audio is off there. The clips
   (`ms1so01`, `csof*`) exist in `language.mix/audio.mix` in the same IMA format
   as the Allied lines that wave 2 verified.

Ghidra was not needed. The building-anim slots, the anim timing, the NewTheater munge and
the frame layout are already recovered in `docs/re/building-render.md` and
`presentation.md`. The undocumented pieces (the infantry Idle/Panic scheduler, the
DamageFire spawner, the rubble draw path and light-source falloff) are listed as RE items for
the cross-cutting fixer.

Harness caveats:
- `w3reveal` raises DiscoveredByPlayer. For example, SOV12 flips "Objective 2 Complete" at f≈50. This affects only the shots.
- `view` clamps at map edges, so some landmark shots are off-centre.
- `--play` runs with audio off, so every speech clip reports "unavailable" there.
