ANDROID TOUCH TEST 27 - YURI FIRING / ANIMATIONS / LAUNCHER

IMPORTANT SAVE COMPATIBILITY
This build uses save format 53 for Gatling configuration/charge and launch
weapon identity/offsets. Format-52 saves from 900021-900026 are not loadable
in this build. Existing save files are not deleted. Keep 900026 available if
you need to finish an older saved match. Start a fresh skirmish for this test.

CHANGES
1. Gatling Tank and Gatling Cannon resolve all three original numbered
   ground/air weapon pairs, normal and elite thresholds, RateUp and RateDown.
   Sustained engagement advances stages; idle time reduces charge. RateDown=0
   resets immediately, as documented in the stock rules. Stage changes select
   actual damage, ROF, projectile and firing-effect weapon data together.
   Available multi-frame vehicle/turret/barrel HVA art animates while firing
   with a stage-dependent cadence. This does not invent frames missing from
   the supplied assets. Building HVA variants use the existing bounded cache.

2. Chronosphere/superweapon shell animation now uses the original art order:
   idle -> opening -> ready loop -> closing -> idle. Opening and closing are
   played once. Readiness is checked for the building's own SuperWeapon type,
   rather than whichever power a house's legacy single-power accessor returns.
   Reverse one-shot animation and stable-loop phase handling were corrected.

3. Physical projectile launch now agrees with the visual lateral muzzle
   offset, alternating burst barrels and turret origin along body direction.
   Building projectile slots receive art muzzle offsets. Building firing
   effects use art FLH instead of a fixed 20-pixel centre offset. Bunker
   occupant firing effects use the occupant's muzzle geometry.
   Projectiles retain the weapon identity captured at launch so their rendered
   sprite/voxel does not change if the shooter changes stage or is removed.

4. Slave Miners seek closer deployment positions (two cells instead of eight).
   Deployed miners check nearby reachable ore rather than any ore in a broad
   square. Loaded worker cargo still prevents packing until it is returned.
   Workers following a packed miner retry a blocked route on a staggered
   cadence. Explicit Stop/manual hold is preserved.

5. Tank Bunker entry/exit receives a short presentation-only interpolation
   between the approach/exit position and bunker centre. Occupant remains
   layered between rear and front bunker art. Gatling occupants advance their
   weapon stages while engaging. This is a visual correction, not a claim of
   pixel-perfect original timing; test different vehicle sizes and facings.

6. AI economic recovery: a house with zero living harvesters may spend its
   credit reserve to buy a replacement instead of holding that money while
   its income is gone. Applies to Allied and Soviet houses. This is a targeted
   recovery fix, not proof of original-game AI strength or complete naval AI.

7. Launcher: prominent full-width Play action, readiness banner, grouped
   file/report controls, dark cards, 56dp minimum button height and full-page
   scrolling. Import/launch/report implementations stay in the existing app.
   Inspired by the Generals X launcher's separation of setup and diagnostics;
   no Generals artwork or source was copied. Display/touch options remain in
   the game Options menu; no nonfunctional renderer settings were added.

8. Sampled reports now include Gatling charge, stage and active weapon IDs.
   Existing 900026 parachute AA and turretless muzzle fixes are retained.

9. All shared shell text now prefers the original Unicode game.fnt UI face
   from the user's files, with yellow text and a dark drop shadow. The classic
   12metfnt face remains a fallback. UTF-8 labels are decoded as codepoints
   rather than individual bytes. This applies through the shared painter to
   main-menu headings, campaign, skirmish, options and dialogs; the original
   animated button artwork keeps its already-baked labels without overdraw.
   The original-font glyph shapes were previewed locally. Full phone visual
   comparison against the user's YouTube reference remains a device test.

PHONE TEST PLAN
- Start a fresh Yuri skirmish. Fire one Gatling Tank continuously at a durable
  ground target, then an aircraft. Observe sound/effect/ROF stage changes.
  Repeat with a powered Gatling Cannon; check cooldown after target loss.
- Charge a Chronosphere and another Allied superweapon. Leave them idle and
  confirm the shell opens once and remains ready; fire and confirm one close.
- Fire Rhino/Lasher/Prism tanks at several headings. Check barrel exit, burst
  side, turret/body offset and projectile flight, including on high terrain.
- Place a Slave Miner away from ore and with a distant ore field in range.
  Check packing, movement, closer deployment, loaded worker return and Stop.
- Enter/evacuate different tanks through a bunker. Check interpolation,
  occupant visibility/layers and firing. Check a Gatling Tank inside as well.
- Run Soviet vs Allied AI at equal difficulty and starting money; share the
  game report with the observed economy/attack issue and faction named.
- Test launcher in portrait/landscape and with larger Android font scaling.
- Save/load during Gatling fire; compare the stage before and after loading.

LIMITATIONS
Device visual/performance testing requires a connected phone. Automated
simulation/compile tests do not establish visual parity or GPU frame pacing.
SpyPlane, ForceShield, GeneticConverter and PsychicDominator were still logged
as unsupported in 900026; their gameplay is not implemented by this patch.
Terrain deformation, full slope smoothing, all particles and all faction AI
are not comprehensively implemented or verified by these targeted changes.
Some firing weapons are instant-hit by their original projectile definition;
do not add visible cannonballs to lasers or invisible bullets arbitrarily.
