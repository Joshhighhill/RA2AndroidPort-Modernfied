Additional user request: two-finger pinch/spread zoom
--------------------------------------------------

clamps. It scales the battlefield and sidebar together. Pinches are camera
events, not simulation commands, and cannot turn into a click on finger lift.

their tests. Exact phone feel and pinch during simultaneous UI overlays still
need device testing. No new render backend or worker-thread claim is made.
