LOCAL DATA AND DIAGNOSTICS
The app stores imported files, settings, saves and diagnostic logs on your
device. Android backup settings may back up internal app data; imported game
archives in external app storage are excluded by the app's backup rules.
Diagnostics may include device/OS/build details, file paths, map/session names,
unit state and error information. Reports are shared only when you request
sharing and choose a destination in Android's share sheet. Check the report and
your description before sharing; do not include passwords or purchase details.
The app does not request proof-of-purchase or upload your game archives.
Multiplayer uses network connections when you choose to play online or on LAN;
this is separate from diagnostic sharing.
